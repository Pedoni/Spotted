Android adaptive icon:
Use android-foreground as the foreground layer and android-background as the background layer.
For Android 13 themed icons, use android-monochrome as the monochrome layer.
The foreground artwork is intentionally kept within the central safe area.

Notes:
SVGs are the vector masters. PNGs are 1024x1024 exports.
The splash PNG/SVG is a transparent logo lockup; place it over your app's splash background.
